WP Forge Companion

Safer updates for your WordPress site

WP Forge Companion is a small plugin your website maintainer installs so they can keep your site up to date without putting it at risk. Every update is tried on a private copy of your site first, and only what works reaches your real site.

What it does

Tests updates first

Your maintainer makes a private copy of your site and tries each update there before anything changes for your visitors.

Keeps restore points

Before any change, your site is saved, so it can be put back exactly as it was in one step.

Changes only what passed

Only the updates that were tested and worked are applied to your real site, and nothing else is touched.

Keeps a way in

If another plugin ever breaks your site, your maintainer can still reach it and fix it quickly.

Reports on site health

Your maintainer can see a read-only summary of your site's health and what needs updating.

Why it is safe to have on your site

  • It only answers your maintainer.Every request must carry your maintainer's private signature. Anything else is turned away.
  • It never contacts anyone on its own.No tracking, no advertising, no outside services. It sends nothing anywhere by itself.
  • It needs no passwords.It does not use or store anyone's WordPress login.
  • Your visitors won't notice it.It doesn't change how your site looks or works, and there is nothing for you to set up.
  • It removes itself cleanly.If it is deleted, it takes its settings and temporary files with it.

How an update works

  1. Your maintainer makes a private copy of your site.
  2. The updates are installed on that copy and its pages are checked.
  3. A restore point of your real site is saved.
  4. Only the changes that passed are applied to your real site. While they go in, visitors may briefly see a short maintenance message.
  5. If anything looks wrong afterwards, your site can be put back as it was.

About your site's data

To test updates, a copy of your site is kept by your website maintainer, on their own computer. WP Forge has no online service that your site is uploaded to, and the copy is not shared with anyone else.

If you have any questions about your site or this plugin, please contact your website maintainer.
